# Regional Returns — European Union

Document owner: Regional Customer Policy
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Scope: Fictional Negativex EU distance-purchase operations

## Scope and ownership

This internal operating reference describes how EU-jurisdiction distance purchases are classified. It is not legal advice. The case record identifies customer location, order channel, product type, and event date before a statutory rule is selected.

## Withdrawal clock

For an in-scope distance purchase, the withdrawal period generally begins when the consumer or a nominated third party receives the goods. Review the order confirmation, carrier delivery event, and any split shipment together. A support contact date is not substituted for the delivery event.

## Standard goods

The regional baseline is a 14-day withdrawal period, subject to documented exceptions. Record whether the goods were used, returned, or altered. The public direct-sale window may differ in another context; this regional source governs when jurisdictional conditions are met.

## Exceptions

Personalized goods may fall outside the ordinary process when customization was made to the customer’s specification. Sealed software can have a separate exception after the seal is broken. Describe the actual product condition rather than relying on an item label.

## Digital entitlements

A downloadable entitlement is assessed with the Digital Goods Policy. Download and activation are different events. If a license was activated, record the activation timestamp and check whether a signed enterprise term changes the result.

## Premium and marketplace interaction

A Premium plan does not by itself establish a regional entitlement. A marketplace order is processed through the marketplace channel. Link the channel record and regional assessment so a plan benefit is not mistaken for a statutory baseline.

## Enterprise terms

A signed enterprise agreement or amendment may contain a negotiated remedy. Contract Operations verifies the contract identifier, amendment number, approval record, and effective date. A public policy is context only until contract scope is established.

## Evidence and escalation

Keep the order confirmation, delivery event, product condition, customer request, and jurisdictional rationale. Escalate uncertain statutory scope to the regional policy owner. Related references include the Enterprise Contract Guide and Returns Operations Manual.
